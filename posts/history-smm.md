## Research and Money
I forget who said this *"research is not free. You only can do the research that is funded."* Much of this works is Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPT) work. It is an FFRDC operated Caltech for NASA. Research on topologic structure of redundent maipulators wasn't diffuse interests, it was a statutory mandate. I guess due to cold war, USA and Soviet Union had a space competition. Public Law 98-371, dated 18 July 1984, directed the NASA Advanced Technology Advisory Committee to study automation and robotics for the Space Station and ATAC reported to Congress in April 1985 (NASA TM-87566), with a further legal requirement to report progress semiannually. All of reports ran into the mid-90s. Congress's stated intent was to build an A&R technology base that would also act as a visible stimulus to the U.S. economy which is exactly why the Tech Brief / commercialization channel was so heavily used (JPL later did a formal redundant-manipulator tech transfer deal with Robotics Research Corp).

Unfortunately, this kind of research was stopped at mid-90s. The Flight Telerobotic Servicer (FTS) was terminated in 1991 amid Space Station Freedom (SSF) budget cuts. The funding line that sustained the topological program evaporated, which is why the literature thins out sharply after ~1997 with the topology of self-motion manifolds under joint limits never really finished. **I probably can keep pushing forward this research line during my Ph.D**.
